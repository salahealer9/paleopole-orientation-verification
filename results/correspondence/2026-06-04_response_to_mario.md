Dear Mr. Buildreps,

Thank you for this. Your message means a great deal, and the willingness to engage in good faith while disagreeing on substance is, as you say, rarer than it should be. I have appreciated this exchange throughout.

I should be transparent about one thing before this chapter closes properly. In the days since the v2 publication, a post-publication peer review identified two further methodological controls I had not yet applied: a latitude look-elsewhere correction (parallel to the longitude control I did run), and a spatial-autocorrelation control that treats spatially clustered sites as a single independent unit rather than as separate observations. The reviewer's points were well-founded; I ran both controls this week.

The findings materially attenuate the v2 conclusion about Pole III. Under the additional controls, the apparent concentration at the Pole III latitude is not distinguishable from what the null produces by chance — the surviving signal in v2 was an artifact of treating spatially autocorrelated sites (about half of the apparent contributors, from a cluster of Mesoamerican structures in particular) as independent observations. Pole II's attenuation continues.

I will be depositing this as version 3 of the Zenodo record within the next 24-48 hours, with your v2 Appendix A commentary preserved verbatim as before. I will send you the v3 DOI when it is available.

I want to be clear about how this relates to the spirit of your message. The methodological discovery is consistent with what we discussed during the v2 comment window: that the analysis would only ever be as good as the controls applied to it, and that legitimate questions could be raised about which controls were appropriate. The post-publication reviewer identified two such questions. Running their suggested controls is the continuation of the same investigative habit that produced the v2 diagnostic of the 26-sigma artifact, not a different one.

Whatever the v3 findings, the procedural respect runs both ways. You shared your data for independent verification under terms that made the verification possible. The discussion that has taken place, both during the formal comment window and through the post-publication review, is what good-faith engagement looks like. I am glad to have done this with someone who, as you put it, took the data seriously rather than waving it away.

If our paths do cross again, I would welcome that too.

With respect,
