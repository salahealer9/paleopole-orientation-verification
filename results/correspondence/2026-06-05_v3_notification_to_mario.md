As I mentioned yesterday, version 3 of the report has now been deposited on Zenodo. The new version DOI is:

https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20546301

The substantive change is the withdrawal of the version 2 finding that clustering at Poles II and III was robust. Under the two additional controls described in §3.8 of v3, a latitude look-elsewhere correction analogous to the meridian correction already in the protocol, and a spatial-cluster null treating spatially clustered structures as one independent unit, the apparent concentration does not survive. The mechanism is set out in §3.8 and §4.7: chiefly, that the site-level signal was inflated by treating a single 29-site Mesoamerican cluster in the northern Yucatán (and other dense same-tradition clusters) as independent observations.

Your version 2 Appendix A commentary remains preserved verbatim. The v2 → v3 relationship is set out in Appendix B. The post-publication review that identified the two controls is summarised in Appendix C, written by the reviewer itself.

The corrected conclusion is that no proposed pole shows clustering distinguishable from chance. As before, this bears only on the orientation-clustering claim; the broader geophysical question is outside the scope of an orientation-clustering analysis.

Thank you again for the engagement throughout this process.
